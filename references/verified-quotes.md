# Verified Quote Bank

This is the **only** list the skill may quote Musk from verbatim. Every Tier 1 line was checked in October 2026 against a primary source: the full transcript, the document or court exhibit, or the archived post itself. Where sources disagreed or only a summary was available, the line went to Tier 2. Last updated 2026-10-16.

**Source tags**
- **[O]**: official or human-made transcript, or the original document (email, court exhibit, his blog post, a published paper).
- **[P]**: his own X/Twitter post, read on an archive copy (PolitiTweet, Wayback copies of Nitter/xcancel).
- **[A]**: a machine transcript checked against the audio context, sometimes against a second transcript too. Fillers like "um" are removed; the words are otherwise his.
- **[R]**: a reputable outlet quoting him directly. No transcript was available.

**Rules**
- **Tier 1:** quote verbatim, with venue and year. Keep any context note that comes with the quote. Several lines mean something different without it.
- **Tier 2:** never put these in quotation marks as his words. Paraphrase them ("he has said, roughly, that…").
- **Anything not on this page:** paraphrase it, or leave it out. The topic reference files contain many more quotes. Those are research evidence, not lines to reuse.
