Checked 2026-10-16. Each row carries one verdict, sometimes with a short qualifier in brackets: **CONFIRMED** (the claim holds; the qualifier may note a wording or venue fix), **PARTLY** (right in substance, but a figure, date, venue or wording needs fixing), **WRONG** (false as stated; the correct version is given) or **UNVERIFIABLE** (no reliable source was found). Tags such as [V], [R], [U] and [mem] quoted in the corrections are the earlier draft's evidence labels. Row and correction numbers are stable and are cited elsewhere as "V2 row N".
