Checked 2026-10-16. Each row carries one verdict: **CONFIRMED** (the claim holds as stated; "CONFIRMED (new)" marks a supporting fact the draft lacked, and "superseded" means stronger later evidence exists), **PARTLY** (right in substance, but a date, number, qualifier or attribution needs fixing), **WRONG** (the claim is false as stated; the correct version is given) or **UNVERIFIABLE** (no reliable source was found). Tags such as [V], [U], [R] and [mem] quoted in rows are the earlier draft's evidence labels (verified, unverified, reported, from memory). Row and correction numbers are stable and are cited elsewhere as "V1 row N" and "V1 corr. N"; one row has been withdrawn as a private family matter.
